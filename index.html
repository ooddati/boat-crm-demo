About BOAT

Education planning with structure, not pressure.

BOAT Education Group Inc. is a Canada-focused education consultancy supporting students with course exploration, institutional selection, application preparation, and enrolment coordination.

We bring the important information into one conversation so students can make decisions with greater confidence. Our approach combines local support, Canadian education knowledge, and clear communication throughout the process.
